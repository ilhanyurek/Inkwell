Inkwell opens and edits office files on your phone. When you save, it keeps the parts it cannot edit, such as charts and equations.

<b>Documents (DOCX, DOC, ODT, TXT, Markdown)</b>
• Bold, italic, underline, headings, lists, quotes, alignment, find and replace
• Tables and pictures appear in place; edit tables and insert new ones
• Links, equations and styles are kept when you save
• Export to PDF or print

<b>Spreadsheets (XLSX, XLS, ODS, CSV)</b>
• Cell formatting: colours, fill, borders, alignment, number and date formats
• 50+ formulas: VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, SUMIFS, dates, TEXT and more
• Sort, fill down, insert and delete rows and columns, multiple sheets
• View charts, freeze panes and filter rows
• Merged cells and conditional formatting are kept

<b>PDF</b>
• Highlights and notes you can remove later
• Draw your signature and place it on the page
• Fill in forms
• Redaction: blur or black out parts of a page, and Inkwell deletes the hidden content from the file
• Select and copy text, jump through the table of contents
• Rotate, delete and extract pages, merge files, search, password-protected files

<b>Presentations (PPTX, ODP)</b>
• View and present slides with speaker notes
• Edit slide text; add, duplicate, move and delete slides
• Create new presentations and export to PDF

<b>Tools</b>
• 29 tools that work offline: merge, split, organize, rotate and delete pages
• Compress and repair PDFs
• Images, camera scans, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and text to PDF
• PDF to images, Word, PowerPoint and text
• Watermark, page numbers, password protect and unlock, crop, flatten, properties, extract images, compare two PDFs

<b>And</b>
• Launcher shortcuts for new files, scanning and your last file
• Undo and redo everywhere
• Drafts are saved automatically and restored after a crash
• Folders tab, thumbnail grid, pinned and recent files
• 22 languages, light and dark theme, icon that follows your wallpaper
• Free and open source (GPL-3.0), without ads or tracking
